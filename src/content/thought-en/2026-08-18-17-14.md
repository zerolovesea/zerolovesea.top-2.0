I used to have a bit of a code-related obsession: if I saw a Claude bot among the contributors to a GitHub project, I’d immediately assume the product was unreliable. I’d think it might have been made by a developer who had just learned how to vibe-code and didn’t really understand software engineering or design patterns. For some reason, that gave me a vague sense of superiority.

Obviously, this kind of bias is narrow-minded. And I’m not exactly a brilliant programmer myself. So when I encountered AI that was both incredibly fast and genuinely effective, I quickly accepted it.
